Conveyancing Services Brisbane Costs Explained

Brisbane Conveyancers describes routine professional fees as typically a few hundred to around a thousand dollars, with many solicitors offering a fixed-fee quote. That professional fee is only one part of the total. Government charges, searches and transfer duty are set out separately, in writing, before you commit.

The published material separates three cost layers:

  • The professional fee for the solicitor's legal work, often quoted as a fixed amount for routine matters.
  • Government charges, including transfer duty calculated on the property and registration fees.
  • Disbursements, such as title searches and property searches, which vary by property.

Two firms can look close on price while taking very different positions on search costs, pre-signing advice or settlement administration. Questions That Reveal the Real Scope

A short quote with no workflow detail can hide delays later. Before comparing prices, confirm what the quoted work actually covers:

  • Check whether pre-signing contract review is included, or billed separately.
  • Ask which searches are expected on the file and whether their cost is inside the quote.
  • Confirm whether PEXA electronic settlement administration is part of the quoted work.
  • Clarify how transfer duty calculation and payment arrangements are handled.
  • Ask who liaises with your lender and the other side, and at which dates.

The safe approach is to treat the dollar figure as the last thing you compare, after the written scope matches your matter.

Timing Pressures That Change the Brief

Cost and timing interact. Most Queensland residential contracts carry a 5 business day cooling-off period starting when the contract is signed by both parties and received. Ending the contract during that period can bring a penalty of up to 0.25% of the purchase price. That makes early review important where finance dates, special conditions or search results may affect the decision to proceed.

Auction purchases have no cooling-off period, so contract review should happen before bidding, not after. Off-the-plan files need careful reading of sunset clauses and disclosure rules for property bought before construction is finished. Each scenario can change the hours a solicitor must commit, which flows through to scope even where the headline fee is fixed.

How Matter Type Moves the Price

The published service list spans 13 service types across 26 Brisbane suburbs, and scope differs by matter:

  • Buyers: contract review, searches, transfer duty, lender liaison and settlement.
  • Sellers: contract preparation, mandatory Form 2 seller disclosure and settlement.
  • First home buyers: transfer duty concessions, the First Home Owner Grant and settlement steps.
  • Family title transfers: transfer documents and duty-related administration even without an open market sale.
  • Commercial parties: leases, GST, due diligence and commercial settlement.

For first home buyers, the Queensland First Home Owner Grant is currently $30,000 towards an eligible new home valued under $750,000, for contracts signed between 20 November 2023 and 30 June 2026, and is scheduled to revert to $15,000 from 1 July 2026. Treat that figure as a starting point for eligibility checks, not a budget assumption.

Who This Applies To and What to Prepare

This guide is most useful for Brisbane buyers and sellers deciding whether the matter is straightforward or needs earlier legal attention. It is especially relevant for auction buyers, off-the-plan purchasers, sellers who need Form 2 disclosure prepared, first home buyers checking concessions, families arranging a title transfer, and parties dealing with commercial premises.

Before requesting a quote on conveyancing services brisbane pricing, prepare the facts that affect scope: state whether you are buying or selling, whether a lender is involved, whether the contract is already signed, whether the property is residential or commercial, and whether timing pressure comes from auction, disclosure preparation or pre-construction terms. Fuller information gives you a faster, more accurate fixed-fee response.

  1. Define your matter. Note whether you are buying or selling, the property type, lender involvement and whether any contract is already signed.
  2. Request a written split. Ask each solicitor to list the professional fee, government charges and disbursements as separate items, with excluded work stated.
  3. Confirm scope items. Check pre-signing review, expected searches, transfer duty handling and PEXA settlement are inside the quoted work.
  4. Factor in timing. Flag auction, cooling-off or off-the-plan deadlines so the solicitor can confirm the file is routine before the dates pass.
  5. Compare like with like. Only compare quotes where the written scope matches, then engage the solicitor whose stated work fits your matter.

Common questions

How much does conveyancing cost in Brisbane? Professional fees for a routine Brisbane conveyance are typically a few hundred to around a thousand dollars, and many solicitors offer a fixed-fee quote. Government charges and disbursements such as title searches and registration are additional and vary by property. Always confirm in writing what is included before you engage anyone.

What does a conveyancer do when buying a house in Queensland? They review your contract, carry out title and property searches, advise on conditions and dates, arrange transfer duty, liaise with your lender and the other side, and manage settlement so ownership transfers correctly. In Queensland this is legal work, carried out by a Queensland Law Society-member solicitor.

How long is the cooling-off period in Queensland? Most residential contracts have a 5 business day cooling-off period, starting when you receive the contract signed by both parties. It does not apply to property bought at auction, and a penalty of up to 0.25% of the purchase price may apply if you terminate during cooling-off.
